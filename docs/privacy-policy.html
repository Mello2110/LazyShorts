LazyShorts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 9, 2026

Overview

LazyShorts is a browser extension that automatically advances to the next YouTube Short when the current one finishes playing. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

In short: LazyShorts does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Collection

LazyShorts does NOT collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Browsing history or activity
  • YouTube viewing habits
  • IP addresses or location data
  • Any analytics or usage statistics

Data Storage

LazyShorts stores only your preferences locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API:

  • Enabled/Disabled state - Whether auto-skip is turned on
  • Delay setting - Seconds to wait before skipping (0-5)
  • Theme preference - Light, dark, or auto mode
  • Skip counter - Number of Shorts automatically skipped

This data is stored using chrome.storage.sync, which may sync across your devices if you're signed into Chrome. This data never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to any external servers.

Network Requests

LazyShorts makes zero network requests. The extension works entirely offline after installation. We do not communicate with any servers, APIs, or third-party services.

Permissions Explained

LazyShorts requests minimal permissions:

  • storage - To save your preferences locally
  • activeTab - To interact with the current YouTube Shorts page
  • Host permission (youtube.com) - To run on YouTube Shorts pages only

We follow the principle of least privilege and only request permissions that are absolutely necessary for the extension to function.

Third-Party Services

LazyShorts does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks. There are no embedded trackers or external scripts.

Children's Privacy

LazyShorts does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal data from anyone, this applies to all users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
